Cyber safety lab

Field notes every digital detective should know.

✉️Pause before you clickUnexpected urgency, strange senders, and requests for credentials can indicate phishing.
🔗Inspect the real domainLook for misspellings, misleading subdomains, and unexpected redirects. HTTPS alone does not prove trustworthiness.
🔐Build better passwordsUse long, unique passwords or passphrases and a reputable password manager. Enable multifactor authentication.
💸Question surprise prizesNever pay a fee or share an OTP to claim an unexpected prize. Verify requests through official channels.
🛡️Protect your accountsKeep software updated, turn on MFA, and avoid reusing passwords across sites.
📣Report suspicious activitySave evidence safely and report suspicious messages to the relevant platform or organization.
